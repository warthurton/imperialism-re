# The map in memory (decoded 2026-11-01 from the running game, fixture `play_fall1817`)

`tools/readmap.py` reads it (read-only); `docs/snapshots.md` covers the raw capture. Global `g_MapMgr` = pointer at 0x6a43d4.

* **Grid**: 6,480 tiles = **108 wide x 60 high**, `index = y * 108 + x` (the code divides and takes the remainder by 0x6c everywhere; rendering the grid gives a recognisable world with mountain ranges and coasts).
  Tiles are 0x24 bytes at map +0xc; provinces are 120 used records (384 allocated) of 0xa8 bytes at map +0x10.
* **Provinces**: byte +0 and +1 = owning nation (equal for the whole saved game; 0-6 Great Powers, 7-22 minors; the local power is the one in `readcity.py`'s "client powers"), short +4 = the town's tile index,
  +0x0a... = neighbour province indices (0xffff = none), 8 provinces per Great Power and 4 per minor. Tile short +0x14 = province index (0xffff on the sea).
* **Tile fields**
  | Offset | Meaning | Status |

  | +0x13 | kind: 1 dry plains, 2 plantation, 3 open range, 4 horse ranch, 5 farm, 6 orchard, 7 fertile hills, 8 barren hills, 9 mountain, 10 / 11 / 12 oil terrain, 13 scrub forest, 15 hardwood forest | confirmed by cross-tab with the card tables |

* **Rule for autoplay (decided by the user, 2026-11-01)**: when we are just playing the game, use **player-visible information only**: prospected tiles (the `prospected` flag), the deposit icons the toolbar shows, what is on screen.
  The unprospected deposits (and any other hidden data) are readable from memory, and may be used **only to test something specific faster** (a fixture, a decode, a rule check), never while playing. `readmap.py find ... --unprospected` is such a test aid.
* **How it was checked**: per-offset histograms of the 6,480 tiles; the owner map rebuilt from provinces (my country lies at x 44-57, y 39-56 with sea to its west, as on screen); a turn-end diff (152 tiles changed, mostly the
  scrolled window and moving units); the unit pointers of my 7 civilians.
* **Controlled Engineer experiment (2026-11-01)**: one Engineer ordered rail from (47,48) into the empty plain (47,49) for $100 and the turn was ended. Tile diff: (47,48) `+06` 40 -> 44 (adds bit 2, SE) and (47,49) `+06` 0 -> 32 (adds bit 5, NW), the visible mask `+17` changed the same way, `+05` already 4 on the new tile, nothing else on my side changed. This confirms the rail mask and the staggered geometry directly. The unit pointer (`+0x20..0x23`) moved with the Engineer.
  Clicking that Engineer's tile afterwards gave an **empty** construction popup (no depot, no port): a tile the capital already gathers (`+05` = its id) offers nothing to build.
* **Depot experiment (2026-11-01, done)**: the Engineer was deployed to the ungathered farm tile (48,50) (one turn), then the construction popup offered "Depot $2,000 ... will gather grain" (and no port: not coastal). Ordered: $2,000 charged **at once** (79,683 -> 77,683). It took **three End Turns** to finish ("Building a depot, time to completion: 3 months" on the unit's report, which is a click on his tile while he works). Diff at the finish:
  (48,50) `+0x1c` 0 -> **16** (the depot bit) and `+0x05` 255 -> **7** on the depot tile and on its four neighbours that nobody gathered yet ((48,49), (49,50), (47,51), (48,51)); its other two neighbours (47,50), (47,49) stayed with the capital's id 4. So a depot claims its own tile plus unclaimed adjacent land, a new gatherer id (7) is allocated, and the depot claims even though it has **no rail**, so `+0x05` records the claim, not the connection ("connected" must be derived elsewhere). The earlier `+0x0e` flips (1 -> 0 on ~30 tiles in my provinces, 0 -> 1 near the ports) are not structure data; likely visibility/visible-window, undecoded.
* **Connected (decoded 2026-11-01): a depot's "connected" light is a flag on the nation's structure object, recomputed at End Turn by a flood fill over the rail.** Not in the tiles, not in any nation field the snapshot captures.
  * **Where**: each Great Power has an MFC pointer list at nation `+0x898` of 0x50-byte structure objects (vtable 0x66d7c8; created by `0x5145b0` for a depot, `0x5147d0` for a port): `+0x14` tile index, `+0x1a` turn built, `+0x1c` nation, **`+0x4c` connected**, `+0x4d` has a port, `+0x4f` has a depot (`+0x4e` is uninitialised). `tools/readstructs.py` lists them with `connected=0/1`.
  * **Seen**: the same depot at (48,50) read `+0x4c` = 0 in fixture `depot_unconnected` and 1 after rail linked it to the capital (that byte was the only difference on the object). The capital and ports always read 1.
  * **How computed** (`TGreatPower_UpdateStructureConnections`, 0x4db7d0): allocate a 6,480-byte reach map, flood it from the capital over rail (`TGreatPower_FloodFillRail`, 0x4dbac0: follow the six direction bits of tile `+0x06`, only onto tiles whose `+0x04` equals the nation's region field, not the owner), then a depot is connected if `reach[its tile]`, a port if the capital's sea access is usable. `TGreatPower_BuildGatherMap` (0x4dbbb0) then builds the gather map from **connected structures only**, which `TClientGreatPower_vf77` sums for the Transport screen: an unconnected depot gathers nothing, as the manual says.
  * **Proof it is recomputed, not event-driven** (poke test, `tools/poke_tile.py`, hidden-information rule: a test): writing the missing rail bits (`+0x06` and `+0x17`) of (47,49), (48,50) and (47,48) into memory left the flag at 0 immediately, and **one End Turn later it read 1**; removing two of the bits set it back to **0 after the next End Turn** (still 1 right after the poke). So the Engineer's build only changes the rail; the flag follows at turn processing, and the green/red light in the game changes with it (red seen before the link in screenshots; the green light was not captured).
  * **Real play, same result**: depot built (3 turns), then two rail pieces of $100 each laid by Engineers, the second linking (47,48) to (47,49); after the turn `readstructs.py` read `connected=1`.
  * The snapshot oracle does not capture this list, so a differential test of the rail/transport rules would need it added (`tools/snapshot_graph.py`).
* **Open**: the "connected" light colour itself (green was not captured), developed levels, the unit object (type, order, cycle order), what `+0x04` (a region id?) really is; fog for ports and depots; the `+0x0e` flips (visibility?). (The capital's tile is the structure with both port and depot flags and `+0x1a` = 0.)
* **Tile -> screen pixel (calibrated and used for real orders, 2026-11-01)**: the visible window is the tiles whose `+0x10` is not 0xff (stale values stay on tiles that scrolled off); the tile with `+0x10` = 0 is the top-left one (ox, oy). Mirror pixels: `x = 22 + 126*(tx-ox) + 63*((ty&1)-(oy&1))`, `y = 140 + 130*(ty-oy)`; game pixels = `((mx-10.4)/1.993, (my-34.5)/1.993)`. About 8 columns x 6-7 rows are on screen. Checked on two different scroll positions (capital and towns land where the formula says; clicking computed tiles ordered real searches). `tools/maptarget.py prospect` applies it.
